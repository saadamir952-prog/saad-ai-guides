What it is

This is the exact workflow behind the video: Claude does the editing, you do the shooting. Download the Claude app, point it at a folder with three subfolders, give it 5 free tools and 3 prompts — then drop in your raw footage and get back a finished reel: silences cut, subtitles on, music in, formatted for Reels. The whole setup is free. The tools are free and open-source. One honest catch, stated up front: Claude Code itself needs a paid Claude plan — Pro is $20/month. Details at the end.

The voiceover in the video says “raw, references, done” for the folders. Our real folder names are Raw Footage / References / Exports — same meaning, same job.

Step 1 — The app and the folders

  1. Download the Claude app (claude.com) and open the Code tab — that is where Claude sees your files.
  2. Make one folder, call it “AI Editor”, with three subfolders inside: Raw Footage/ (your raw voice takes, raw captures, B-roll), References/ (3–5 reels you like — links and notes only, never another creator’s full video), Exports/ (finished videos only).
  3. Point Claude at that folder so it can see your files. Everything Claude edits lives in this folder — nothing scattered on your desktop.

Step 2 — The 5 tools (all free, $0)

Claude needs these five to actually build a video. Verify each one exists before you start:

  • Remotion 4.0.542 — code-based video: the edit is written as code, then rendered.
  • HyperFrames — screenshot-capture renderer: turns compositions into frames (no version claimed — it is a skill, not a release).
  • FFmpeg 8.1.2 — cutting, loudness normalisation, muxing the final file.
  • faster-whisper 1.2.1 — transcription plus word-level timestamps, so captions land on the right frame.
  • yt-dlp 2026.10.26 — downloading public reference reels to study.

On my machine the voice is also ElevenLabs (voice Will, model eleven_v3, plain — no tags, no cloned voice). That part is optional: if you record your own voiceover, it goes in Raw Footage/ and replaces the TTS.

Step 3 — Taste: teach Claude what you like

Before Claude edits anything, show it what good looks like. Collect 3–5 reels you like — links and short notes in References/: what is the hook, how fast does it cut, what do the captions look like, what does it sound like. Then paste Prompt 2 (below, condensed — the full text lives in the same workflow) and let Claude turn those references into a style guide for your video: one hook template, your visual language, your pacing, your caption style, your SFX character, and exactly what to steal versus what to leave behind.

The line that matters most, word for word from Prompt 2: “Inspire, never copy. A reference is a format + a feeling, not a script to translate.” Never paste another creator’s script. Never copy their exact visuals. Never make a paid tool the subject.

Step 4 — Shoot, then paste Prompt 3

Film your raw footage and drop it in Raw Footage/. Then paste Prompt 3 below. Claude cuts the silences, adds subtitles, adds music, formats for Reels — and when something is off, you fix it in plain words, like “make the hook bigger”. No timeline scrubbing, no re-editing from scratch.

Real numbers from our own run, not the reference video’s: our silence-removal script cut 19 pauses, −4.8 seconds from a scratch copy of the voiceover. The reference creator said “17 pauses cut” — that was his video, not this workflow.
