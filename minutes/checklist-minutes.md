# The 32-Second Edit — Checklist

Free from Saad AI (@saad.aig). The step-by-step checklist for the edit in
the TIME video: three hours by hand, 32 seconds with Claude.

Full guide: https://saadamir952-prog.github.io/saad-ai-guides/minutes/

---

## Before you start

- [ ] Your raw clips are in ONE folder (no bins, no timeline, no prep).
- [ ] Claude Code is installed and you have a paid Claude plan.
- [ ] You know your target length (e.g. under 30 seconds for a Reel).

## The edit

- [ ] 1. DROP — Put the clips in the folder. The folder IS the project.
- [ ] 2. PICK — Watch the clips once. Keep the takes you want, drop the
      rest. (Claude does not pick takes — the choice is yours.)
- [ ] 3. TYPE — One line in Claude Code:
      `Edit my video: cut silences, add captions, export vertical.`
- [ ] 4. WALK AWAY — The run takes seconds. Come back when it's done.

## Check the 3 jobs

- [ ] SILENCES CUT — No dead air in the voiceover. Play it back: does
      the voice flow without awkward pauses?
- [ ] CAPTIONS ON — Word-by-word captions, timed to the speech, readable
      on a phone screen.
- [ ] SOUND MASTERED — The voice is clear and loud enough; no clipping,
      no hiss, no sudden volume jumps.

## Before you post

- [ ] Watch the export ONCE, start to finish, with sound on.
- [ ] Anything wrong? Fix it with ONE more line
      (e.g. `Make the captions bigger` or `Cut the last 2 seconds`).
- [ ] Re-export and watch the fix.
- [ ] Post it. (Colour grading is NOT part of this workflow — if your
      footage needs a look, grade it yourself first.)

## Measured times (the run in the video)

- Cut the silences: 7.2 s
- Generate the captions: 5.2 s
- Master the sound + export: 19.5 s
- TOTAL: 32.1 s (shown as 00:32 in the video)

Your times will vary with clip length and machine speed. The shape
holds: the mechanical jobs take seconds, and the hours disappear.
