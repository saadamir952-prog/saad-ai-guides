The 4 steps

  1. Stakes — someone to root for, something at risk, and a reason it matters NOW. Our example: “One AI tool a day, chasing 10K in 30 days.”
  2. Big question — ONE question the viewer needs answered. The classic form: “Is that even possible?”
  3. Twist — the viewer expects one thing; you reveal something different. They expect trending audio or crazy editing — the real secret is studying proven frameworks.
  4. Re-hook — answer one question, then immediately open another, so the video never feels finished until you decide it is.
