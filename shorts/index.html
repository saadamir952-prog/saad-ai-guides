The 5 steps

  1. Reads the transcript — Claude transcribes the whole video with a timestamp for every word — the timing that makes word-by-word captions possible.
  2. Scores the best moments — Claude reads the full transcript and scores 8–12 candidate segments — anything below 60/100 is dropped.
  3. Cuts them vertical — Each approved segment becomes a 15–55 s vertical clip, reframed to follow the speaker's face.
  4. Adds animated captions — Remotion renders word-by-word animated captions in one of 3 styles — Bold, Bounce, or Clean.
  5. Exports for every platform — One render per platform — YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels, each with its own encoding preset.
