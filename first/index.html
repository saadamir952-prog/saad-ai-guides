The first prompt (copy-paste)

Edit this like a finished reel: cut the pauses, add captions, level the sound, export vertical.

What to shoot first

Shoot these three, 5–10 seconds each, phone vertical, good light:

1. Your face, one sentence. Say the one thing your video is about.
2. Your hands doing the thing. The product, the tool, the food.
3. The result. The finished thing, close up, held still 3 seconds.

Talk like you talk. Pauses are fine — the edit cuts them.

The honest limits

Your first video will look finished. It won't make you a filmmaker overnight, and it can't fix a clip that's too dark or too quiet. Shoot the result shot well and the rest follows.
